When can I claim my German pension refund?

The short answer. You can apply on the first day of the 25th calendar month after the last month in which you paid compulsory German pension contributions. If your last contribution month was March 2024, the first day to apply is 1 April 2026. This is general information. The German pension insurance (Deutsche Rentenversicherung) decides every application.

Where the 24 months come from

The law says contributions are refunded only if 24 calendar months have passed since you left compulsory insurance, and compulsory insurance has not started again (§ 210 (2) SGB VI). The pension fund's English brochure for India says the same: "A waiting period of at least 24 calendar months must be completed" (brochure, PDF page 11).

The clock starts the month after your last contribution month. March 2024 was the last month, so the 24 months are April 2024 to March 2026, and you can apply from 1 April 2026. Germany Pension Refund works through this example in its own article and calculator (Germany Pension Refund).

How to find your last contribution month

Look at your insurance record (Versicherungsverlauf). It shows the last month with compulsory contributions, and that month is the one that counts, according to Germany Pension Refund (article).

That article says three dates do not move the clock: the day you deregistered, the day you left Germany, and the day your employer made the last payroll transfer. It also says two kinds of months can move it later:

  • months on German unemployment benefit (ALG I), which normally count as compulsory-contribution months
  • child-raising periods credited to your record

If either applies to you, take the date from your record.

What restarts the clock

New compulsory insurance restarts the wait. The law says a refund needs that compulsory insurance has not started again (§ 210 (2) SGB VI).

Germany Pension Refund says this covers compulsory insurance in Germany, the EU, the UK, Türkiye and the former Yugoslav states (article).

The pension fund's brochure goes wider. It says insurance liability in an EU member state, or in a country with which Germany has a social security agreement, "can be regarded as equivalent" to German insurance, and then no refund is paid (brochure, PDF page 11). India has such an agreement with Germany. The brochure does not say whether compulsory insurance in India, for example in a provident fund, counts. If it applies to you, ask the pension fund before you apply.

The other conditions

The wait is only one condition. For an Indian citizen, a refund also needs fewer than 60 months with contributions, and no residence in the EU. These come from the pension fund's brochure (PDF page 9). The page Pension refund from Germany for Indian citizens explains them, with the documents and where to send the form.

Can I apply before the 24 months are over?

No. All About Berlin says that if you send the documents too early, they are returned (guide). Germany Pension Refund adds that applying early does not shorten the wait and can lead to extra questions from the pension office (calculator result).

Is there a deadline after the 24 months?

Germany Pension Refund says there is no deadline for a first application. You can apply years later, as long as the conditions still fit on the day you apply. It also says waiting earns nothing, because no interest is paid for the time before you apply (article). The brochure confirms that no interest is paid (PDF page 12).

What if my application is rejected?

Germany Pension Refund says a rejection only reflects your situation on the day you applied. If something changed, for example you moved out of the EU or the 24 months have passed, you can apply again (Germany Pension Refund).

Ways to remember the date

  • A calendar file. The free date checker makes a calendar file (.ics) with alerts 30 days and 7 days before the day. It works with Apple Calendar, Outlook and most phone calendars, and you can add the event to Google Calendar from the same page.
  • An email reminder. All About Berlin's calculator offers an email reminder for the month (calculator).
  • Let a company prepare the claim while you wait. FundsBack says it can check your case when you leave so that "the claim goes out as soon as the wait is over" (FundsBack). Germany Pension Refund says that in a managed claim it prepares your documents during the wait, and its partner law firm submits the application once the wait has ended (article). These services charge a share of the refund, paid only if the refund arrives.

Remember

A refund ends your German pension insurance, and rights from the earlier months lapse (§ 210 (6) SGB VI). The brochure advises asking for advice first, because a pension may be the better choice (PDF page 12).
