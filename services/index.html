Three ways I can help.

Start with the problem you can point to. The first job can be one repair, one page or one repeated task.

  1. 01

    Website repairs

    Make it easier for a visitor to reach you and use the pages you already have.

    • Correct phone, email and enquiry links.
    • Fix broken layouts and unfinished page copy.
    • Check agreed contact paths on desktop and mobile.
    • Review forms and integrations with your permission.
  2. 02

    Business websites & landing pages

    A clear place to explain your services, the area you cover and how a customer can get in touch.

    • Build a focused new site or improve existing pages.
    • Organise services, location details and contact options.
    • Prepare mobile layouts and basic search metadata.
    • Hand over the agreed pages and editing instructions.
  3. 03

    Workflow tools & automation

    Reduce the copying, checking and chasing around one recurring business task.

    • Connect forms, spreadsheets and existing systems.
    • Build small dashboards or enquiry and quote tools.

    • Document the setup, access and recovery steps.

A few useful details.

Can you work with my current website provider?

Yes. A small issue may be something your existing provider can fix. I can document the finding, work within an agreed role or take on the repair when you want outside help.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the platform, access and work needed. I send a written quote before work starts. Extra pages, features or ongoing maintenance need a separate agreement.

Will this put my business at the top of Google?

No ranking position is promised. I can improve page structure, service and location information, and technical basics within the agreed scope. Search placement depends on factors beyond a website change.

What access will you need?

We agree the minimum access needed for the task, including a backup or recovery plan where appropriate. Please do not put passwords, payment information or customer records in the first email.

Do you take international projects?

I am based in Madhya Pradesh, India and work remotely. We agree the communication schedule, project currency and delivery arrangements before starting.
