Website privacy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: creet.in counts visits with Google Analytics so we can see which pages are read and where visitors come from. Nothing from the website is used for advertising or sold. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland get no analytics cookies.

What this covers

This notice covers the pages of the creet.in website, including the product pages under creet.in such as /dispatch-desk/, /itr-desk/, /gstr2b/, /resource-lens/, /returns-portal/ and /pickup-points-for-packeta/. Each product (extension, add-on, plugin or app) has its own privacy policy, linked from its pages, which describes what the product itself does with data. The website is run by an independent developer based in India ("we").

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google, to understand how the website is used. When you load a page, your browser sends Google:

  • the page you viewed, the page or site that sent you there, and any campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source=facebook on a link from an ad). Every other part of the address after the "?" is removed before it is sent, so details such as the payment reference on the page you return to after checkout never reach Google;
  • clicks on links to other websites, such as the Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons, Firefox Add-ons or the checkout page;
  • your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language;
  • an approximate location (country and city), which Google works out from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses;
  • outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a random identifier kept in first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_<ID>, which last up to two years) so that repeat visits are counted once.

We see analytics results as totals and charts. Analytics does not provide us with your name, email or anything you type on another site.

Email enquiries

If you email support@creet.in about a product or service, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer your enquiry and discuss or carry out work you request. Our email providers process the messages to deliver and store them. The services page opens your email app; it does not submit an enquiry through a website form. Please do not include passwords, payment information or customer records in an initial enquiry.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

For visitors in these countries, analytics storage is off by default: no Google Analytics cookies are set and no identifier is stored on your device. Google receives only cookieless pings (for example, that a page was viewed), which it does not link to you. The website has no cookie banner, so this stays off.

What we have switched off

  • Advertising cookies, ad personalisation and Google signals are off for everyone.
  • The website's analytics data is not linked to any advertising account and is not sold.
  • Analytics runs only on creet.in, not on test copies of the site.

How long the data is kept

Google Analytics keeps event-level data for this website for two months, after which only aggregated reports remain.

How to opt out

  • Install Google's Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Or block or delete cookies for creet.in in your browser settings, or use a content blocker.

The website works the same either way.

Other services the website uses

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages serves the website. GitHub may log your IP address for security and operations; see the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Fonts: the pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • Links you follow: store listings, checkout pages and other sites you open from here have their own privacy policies.

For more on how Google uses data from websites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.
