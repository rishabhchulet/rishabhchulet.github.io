1. The service

GSTR-2B Reconciler ("the Add-on") is a Google Sheets editor add-on that reconciles a GSTR-2B downloaded from the GST portal against a purchase register in the same spreadsheet. It is provided by Rishabh Chulet, an independent developer in India (the "Operator"). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Google LLC, the Goods and Services Tax Network, the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs or the Government of India.

2. Free and Pro plans

The free plan is available without payment and reconciles up to 200 invoices a month per Google account, counted as described on the home page. Pro removes the monthly limit and is unlocked with a licence key purchased through Dodo Payments, which acts as merchant of record and is the seller for the transaction. Pro is sold as a one-year licence for a single payment of ₹999, including taxes; it does not renew automatically, and you can buy another year when it ends. A Pro licence is for one practitioner and may be activated on up to 3 Google accounts.

3. Your responsibilities

  • The reconciliation is a working paper to help you review a client's input tax credit. Check the Mismatched and Missing tabs, and the source data, before you claim ITC, file a return or advise a client. You remain responsible for every figure you file.
  • The matching rules, tolerances and column mapping are suggestions. Confirm the mapping for each file; a wrong mapping gives wrong results.
  • You confirm you are authorised to handle the GSTINs, invoices and other client data you put in your spreadsheets, and that you will share those spreadsheets only as your professional obligations allow.
  • Keep your own copies of your source files. The Add-on replaces its own result tabs on every run.
