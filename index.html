How every Creet tool works

The same three rules hold for each tool, whichever portal or form it works on.

  • Your data stays with you

    Client lists, carrier profiles and invoices live in your browser or your spreadsheet. There is no Creet server holding them.

  • You press submit

    The tools fill, sort, match and check. They never log in, file a return or submit a form for you.

  • Free to start

    Each tool has a free plan. Pro is a licence key bought through Dodo Payments, with a 14-day money-back guarantee.
