How every Creet tool works

The same three rules hold for each tool, whichever portal or form it works on.

  • Your data stays with you

    Client lists, carrier profiles and invoices live in your browser or your spreadsheet, and your code never leaves your machine. Returns Portal keeps its return requests on the store's own Wix site. There is no Creet server holding any of it.

  • You press submit

    The tools fill, sort, match, check and suggest. They never log in, file a return, submit a form or change your code without you. Returns Portal refunds nothing until you approve it.

  • Free to start

    Each tool has a free plan, and Resource Lens is free outright. Pro on the Chrome extensions and the Google Sheets add-on is a licence key bought through Dodo Payments, with a 14-day money-back guarantee. Returns Portal's Pro plan is billed by Wix.
